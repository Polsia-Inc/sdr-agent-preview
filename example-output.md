## Annotation: Which Layer Produced What

| Email element | Source field | Layer | Why it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| "Post-Series B SDR ramp" (subject) | enrichment_data.funding_stage + role | L2 | Under 50 chars. No urgency language. Funding + role signal = credible specificity without manufacturing urgency. |
| "Saw the Series B announcement" | enrichment_data.recent_signal `[HOOK: enrichment_data.recent_signal]` | L2 | Verified hook. Source cited in log. Not invented. Passes hallucination check. |
| "congrats on the Sequoia close" | enrichment_data.recent_signal (investor parsed) | L2 | Specific verified detail. Would be absent if signal was null — no fabrication fallback. |
| "most VP Sales I talk to lose 60 days" | ROLE_PAIN_MAP: VP Sales `[HOOK: role.universal_pain]` | L2 | Role-frame match. Not a company-specific claim — avoids hallucination. Resonates because it's true for the role broadly. |
| "already on Apollo and HubSpot" | enrichment_data.tech_stack | L2 | Verified from enrichment. Removes "another tool to evaluate" objection before it forms. |
| Single CTA: "15-minute call this week?" | ROLE_TO_VALUE_MAP: VP Sales CTA style | L2 | Low-friction. Specific. Not "I'd love to connect." |
| Physical address + unsubscribe | CAN-SPAM compliance block | L2 | Auto-appended. Jurisdiction: US. Compliance check: passed. |
| No em-dashes, no banned phrases | Phrase blacklist filter | L2 | "I noticed", "just reaching out", em-dash all absent. Passes AI-detection filter. |
